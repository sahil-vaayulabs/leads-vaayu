This is actually a **good conversation**. The biggest positive sign is that he didn't ask **"Kitne ka hai?"** first. Instead, he asked:

> **"Isse mujhe bade orders milenge kya?"**

That tells you he's thinking about **ROI**, not the website itself.

## On the call, don't sell a website. Sell business growth.

The flow should be:

### 1. Understand his business (5 minutes)

Ask questions like:

* Solar water heater install karte ho ya panel bhi?
* Kis city ya area mein kaam karte ho?
* Ek month mein kitne projects ho jaate hain?
* Average ek project ki value kitni hoti hai?
* Bade orders ka matlab builders/commercial clients ya residential?

This helps you understand whether one extra customer is worth ₹30,000 or ₹5 lakh.

---

### 2. Understand lead sources

Ask:

* Abhi maximum enquiries kahan se aati hain?

  * Google Business?
  * Reference?
  * Facebook?
  * Justdial?

Then ask:

> Agar Google se 10 aur enquiries aa jaye har month, useful rahega?

He'll almost certainly say yes.

---

### 3. Explain the website simply

Don't talk about React, SEO, hosting.

Say something like:

> Website sirf ek online brochure nahi hoti.
>
> Jab koi "solar water heater near me" ya "solar installation Pune" search karta hai, to website ki wajah se aap Google mein aur jagah dikh sakte ho.
>
> Wahan aap apne projects, customer reviews, certificates, brands aur enquiry form dikha sakte ho.
>
> Isse trust bhi badhta hai aur bade clients ko quotation bhejna bhi easy hota hai.

---

### 4. Then ask ONE important question

This is where you understand pricing.

Instead of asking his budget, ask:

> Sir ek average project ki value kitni hoti hai?

If he says

> ₹80,000

then continue

> Agar website se saal bhar mein sirf 3–4 extra projects bhi mil jaye, to investment recover ho jayega na?

Now the discussion becomes about **returns**, not cost.

---

## Should you ask budget?

Don't ask

> "Aapka budget kitna hai?"

It makes it sound like you're trying to price based on his wallet.

Instead ask

> Aap website ko kis level pe banana chahte ho?
>
> Basic company profile ya lead generation focus?

Then tell him you'll recommend the right package.

## My goal for this call

Don't try to close the sale immediately.

Instead, end the call with:

> "Main aapke business ke liye ek homepage ka sample design aur ek plan bana deta hoon. Phir 15 minute baat karke final kar lenge."

That lowers resistance significantly. People are much more likely to buy after they've seen something tailored to their business.

Also, once he tells you:

* the average project value,
* his service area,
* and the type of customers he wants (residential vs. commercial),

you'll be in a much better position to justify a ₹20k–₹30k website rather than negotiating from an arbitrary starting price.
